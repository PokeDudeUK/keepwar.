Feats

Dynasty

Founding a new dynasty resets your level, coins and Armoury ranks. In return every future soldier is 8% stronger and every map pays 10% more coins — permanently, and it stacks.

How to play

Dragfrom your keep to a target to march a share of its army (½ ¾ All toggle up top)
Sweepthrough several of your keeps to attack from all of them
Tap twiceon a keep to upgrade it — bigger keeps breed spearmen, then knights
CountersSpears beat knights, knights beat footmen, footmen beat spears
Pan & zoomDrag empty ground to scroll, pinch to zoom, tap the minimap to jump
Green keepsare bandits. They raid whoever's weakest
FortressesTap twice on a level-3+ keep to raise it (up to six flags — tougher knights), specialise it (Barracks, Archers, Market) or build catapults. It marches with your next order, bombards keeps from range, and needs an escort
BreachA captured keep drops to one flag — its walls are broken in the taking. The Salvage perk keeps more standing.
Coinsfrom every map buy permanent upgrades and abilities in the Armoury

Campaign

Replay beaten maps for half coins, or skip ahead to any map. Every tenth map is a boss citadel.
